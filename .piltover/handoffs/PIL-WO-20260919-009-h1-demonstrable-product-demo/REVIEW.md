# REVIEW — H1 Demonstrable Product Demo

Review state: OPEN

## Review focus

- Does each implementation slice reduce distance to H1 or protect a material invariant?
- Are existing Piltover seams reused before adding new architecture?
- Is Ads integrated with Brand, Strategy, Creative, Performance and Learning rather than implemented as an isolated feature?
- Are tenant, RBAC, approval and external-action boundaries preserved?
- Is runtime evidence used for product claims?
- Does the work stop once H1 is sufficiently demonstrated?

## Rejection triggers

- returning to phase-completeness as the primary delivery objective;
- adding architecture without an immediate H1 consumer;
- claiming real integration from mocks;
- bypassing approval or authority boundaries for demo convenience;
- promoting the experimental delivery philosophy as a stable method without longitudinal evidence.
